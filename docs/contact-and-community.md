# Contact and community invitations

The main navigation uses **Contact Ian** and links to `/contact`. On `/speaking`, the contextual button remains **Book Ian** and links to `/speaking#book`. The footer also links to Contact Ian. The general contact page reuses the project inquiry endpoint with `source=contact`, the four service interests, visible direct email, canonical social links, and an event-booking link.

`src/lib/ian-community.ts` holds two destinations: `IAN_COMMUNITY_PAGE_URL` (`/community`) introduces Ian's free community and the LaunchBox platform he built; `IAN_COMMUNITY_URL` opens **Ian McDonald AI** directly on LaunchBox. The shared public header's Free community link and compact Join free link use the internal overview page. The brand remains **Ian McDonald AI**, and Notes is not in the main navigation.

`CommunityInvitation` provides a fuller invitation before the footer on public pages, including the resource pages. Its main Join my free community button goes directly to LaunchBox, while the secondary Meet the community & the platform I built link opens `/community`. The overview page omits this repeated shared invitation because it has its own community invitation and founder story. The supplier readiness builder and waitlist keep the header invitation but omit the large lower invitation because their fixed sidebars would cover it; the builder also retains its existing footer exclusion. Admin tools, full-screen lesson decks, and LaunchBox's separate newsletter reader retain their own layouts. Existing Summit and class-completion community invitations retain the direct LaunchBox destination.

The invitation offers practical lessons, prompts, questions, and sharing work; it does not promise all paid courses, unlimited AI tools, or a response schedule. Internal community overview links emit `button_click` with `button_name=community_overview` and their placement. The `community_visit` event is reserved for outgoing interest in the LaunchBox community, not a completed signup. Contact submissions emit `generate_lead` only after notification acceptance, with no contact details or project text in Analytics.
