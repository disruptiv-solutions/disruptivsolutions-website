Subject: Help with our workshop follow-up?

Hi,

I run Northstar Training, a small training business for local business owners. We deliver hands-on workshops and one-to-one sessions.

I spend too much time turning workshop notes into resources and answering the same inquiry emails. I would like a simple way to draft materials and follow up consistently, while reviewing everything myself. We already use Gmail, Docs, Drive, Meet, and Calendar. I do not want to add a new CRM.

Could we arrange a discovery call to work out where to start?

Jordan

PART 3 — TRY THE LESSON

1. Generate five discovery questions from the inquiry.
2. Add the notes to the same Gemini conversation.
3. Draft a proposal with unconfirmed details clearly marked.
4. Use your own calendar to propose a future practice time.
5. Draft a follow-up using placeholders. Do not send it.
6. Prepare a private tentative hold, with no guests. Create it only if you choose to.

CHECK YOUR WORK

Did Gemini invent a price, date, website, commitment, or guaranteed saving?
Does the proposal distinguish the agreed first step from the suggested pilot?
Does the email ask whether the time works rather than imply it is confirmed?
